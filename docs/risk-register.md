# TECHNICAL RISK REGISTER & MITIGATION STRATEGY

| Risk ID | Category | Description | Severity | Likelihood | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TR-01** | Acoustic / Generalization | Limited recordings per word may cause poor recognition for unseen speakers. | High | High | Enforce strict speaker-independent evaluation (train/val/test splits strictly separated by Speaker ID). Abstain with `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS` rather than false positives. |
| **TR-02** | Segmentation | Two repetitions in one recording may cause vector contamination if pooled together. | High | High | Implement Silero VAD segmentation with strict duration limits (300ms–4000ms). Treat each repetition as a distinct segment with separate embedding records. |
| **TR-03** | Audio Preprocessing | Spectral noise reduction may strip subtle phonemes (aspirations, retroflex consonants in Badaga/Marwari). | High | Medium | Preserve dual-path processing: generate and index both Original and Denoised embeddings. Candidate fusion via RRF queries both paths simultaneously. |
| **TR-04** | Embedding Semantics | XLS-R 300M is a contextual speech representation model, not an explicit word classifier. | Medium | High | Apply attention-masked mean pooling over non-padded speech frames followed by L2-normalization. Aggregate top-K candidate embeddings per word ID. |
| **TR-05** | Acoustic Ambiguity | Very short words (< 500ms) may cluster tightly in cosine space. | Medium | Medium | Implement margin-based calibration: if top-1 and top-2 cosine distances differ by less than calibrated $\delta$, declare `AMBIGUOUS` and return both candidates. |
| **TR-06** | Dialects | Regional and dialect variations (e.g. Jodhpur vs Khetri for Marwari; Ooty vs Jakkanarai for Badaga). | Medium | Medium | Store speaker dialect/village metadata with audio assets. Display dialect notes alongside verified dictionary meanings. |
| **TR-07** | Data Leakage | Random splitting across recordings could put the same speaker in both reference index and test set. | Critical | High | Mandatory speaker-partitioned evaluation splits in `scripts/evaluate_recognition.py`. |
| **TR-08** | Vector Consistency | Zilliz Cloud and Qdrant are separate cloud services without distributed two-phase commits. | High | Medium | Deterministic UUIDv5 vector IDs computed from `(dataset_version_id + word_id + audio_asset_id + audio_variant)`. Durable outbox pattern with transactional replication tracking in PostgreSQL. |
| **TR-09** | AI Hallucination | Gemini and Groq LLMs may fabricate meanings, grammar rules, or sentences for low-resource languages. | Critical | High | Hard grounding in PostgreSQL. LLM prompts are heavily constrained with retrieved approved records only. Pydantic validation rejects/overwrites non-approved vocabulary entries. |
| **TR-10** | STT / TTS Support | Generic STT (Whisper) or TTS (Piper/eSpeak) lack native Badaga and Marwari models. | High | High | Rule: Native verified audio recordings in object storage are the SOLE authoritative audio playback source. Generic STT is disabled from authoritative recognition. |
| **TR-11** | Memory & Indexing | 1024-dimensional embeddings across dual paths (1200+ vectors) require efficient indexing. | Low | Low | Normalized embeddings with Cosine / Inner Product metric. Load XLS-R model once per worker using `@worker_process_init.connect` in Celery. |
| **TR-12** | Compute Resources | CPU inference latency for XLS-R 300M without GPU acceleration. | Medium | Medium | Cache query embeddings in Redis. Warm model in memory. Quantize to INT8/ONNX or use batched CPU inference when running without GPU. |
